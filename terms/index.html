These terms apply when you book Aditya Chowhan — solo or with his band — on adimakesmusic.in. By booking, you agree to them.

01Booking

Choose a set and its length on the booking page, add your event details and pay online. Your booking is confirmed the moment the payment goes through: the page confirms it, and Razorpay emails a receipt if you gave an email address. Each date takes one booking, so a date that’s already taken can’t be booked again. If we ever can’t perform on your date, you get a full refund.

02Prices and payment

Prices are listed in Indian rupees on the booking page and are per performance, for the length of set you choose; the full amount is paid when you book. We’ll call you before the event to settle the set list, timings on the day and the setup. Online payments are processed by Razorpay; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

03What the venue provides

For band performances, the venue provides the sound setup in our rider: mics and stands, a mixer, amplifiers, XLR cables, DI boxes, stage monitors, a PA system and a sound engineer. We’ll confirm what your space needs before the event.

04On the day

Please share the schedule, a contact person at the venue and any timing changes as early as you can. The set runs for the length you booked.

05Safety

The performers may pause or end a performance if their safety, or the safety of your guests, is at risk.

06Cancellations

Cancellations and refunds are covered by our cancellation & refund policy.

07Liability

We aren’t responsible for losses caused by events outside our control, and our total liability for any booking is limited to the amount paid for it.

08Changes and law

We may update these terms; the version published when you booked applies to your booking. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
