We collect only what we need to book and play your event — and nothing for advertising.

01What we collect

When you send a booking request or pay: your name, your phone or WhatsApp number, your email if you give it, and your event details — date, time, venue, the occasion and anything you tell us.

02Payments

Online payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive confirmation of the payment — amount, payment ID and method — but never your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab) applies to its checkout.

03How we use it

To reply to you, confirm and plan your booking, perform at your event, and keep the records we need for accounts and tax.

04Who sees it

Aditya, the musicians playing your event and, where the setup needs it, your venue. Messages travel through WhatsApp and Gmail. We don’t sell or rent your information.

05On this website

The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It remembers one thing, in your own browser: whether you’ve paused the animations. Our host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs, and Razorpay’s checkout may set its own cookies when you pay.

06How long we keep it

For as long as your booking needs, and afterwards only as long as the law requires for our accounts.

07Your choices

Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
