Plans change. Here's what happens to the money if they do.

01If we can't take your date

Booked dates can’t be booked twice, but if two payments ever land on the same date, the later one gets a full refund.

02If we cancel

If we have to cancel a confirmed booking, you get a full refund.

03If you cancel

  • 30 days or more before the event — a full refund.
  • 7 to 29 days before — a 50% refund.
  • Less than 7 days before — no refund.

04How refunds work

Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your payment ID. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days; your bank may take a few more days to show it.
